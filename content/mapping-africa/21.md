By the 1820s, European fascination with Africa had shifted from coastlines to the interior, and the story of Mungo Park, the Scottish explorer of the Niger River, captivated the public. This map highlights his celebrity status across the continent’s middle, where “Park’s Discoveries” draws attention to his 1795-96 route out and return in red, as well as his fatal 1805-06 expedition in blue. Park is believed to have drowned at the Niger’s Bussa rapids after a conflict with local people, but the account was met with some skepticism in Europe, and the mystery of his fate was still alive when this map was published. Around Park’s routes the map shows what Europeans knew, or thought they knew, about the interior: deserts, quicksand, gum forests, caravan tracks dotted with settlements, and the territories of the region’s many peoples.
