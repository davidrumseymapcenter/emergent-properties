The only known copy of this untitled pictorial map shows Southern Africa in the midst of the Second Boer War, fought between the British Empire and the Boer settler republics of the Transvaal and Orange Free State over African land and its rich gold resources. Though remembered as a war between white powers, Black South Africans served and suffered in it by the tens of thousands. The map itself is a patriotic muscle-flex for the British public, showing Queen Victoria at the top and center, with commanders Roberts and Kitchener, fresh from victories in the top left corner. Bird’s-eye views of Cape Town, Johannesburg, and other settlements dot the vivid green landscape which conspicuously contains no representation of indigenous inhabitants.
