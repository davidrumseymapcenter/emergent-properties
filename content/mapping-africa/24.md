What would Africa look like if Europe had never colonized the continent? Swedish artist Nikolaj Cyon asks us to imagine exactly that in this map of West Africa as it might have appeared in 1853, a generation before the Berlin Conference carved up the continent between European powers. Nearly every cartographic convention represented elsewhere in this section is deliberately challenged. South is oriented at the top, places are labeled with local endonyms rather than European placenames, and the title, written in Yoruba, translates as “Western Alkebu-lan,” a reference to what some scholars believe to be an indigenous pre-colonial name for Africa.
