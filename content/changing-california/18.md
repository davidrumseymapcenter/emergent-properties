Three and a half centuries after Briggs, California again floats free from the continent, this time by choice. David Schiller, one of the kings of 1960s rock art who created album covers and posters for psychedelic rock bands, drew the state through the lens of its own counter culture, depicting it as a sort of island utopia detached from the rest of the world. His exaggerated landscape celebrates California’s striking natural diversity, from the jagged Sierra Nevada to the coastal redwood forests, and is packed with the era’s famous venues and landmarks. Originally created for Jefferson Airplane, the map shows the band flying over the mountains and scattering flowers in its path.
