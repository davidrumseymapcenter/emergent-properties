This ship’s log shows how position-finding worked in practice on a Victorian-era ship. The left page records hourly readings of course, speed, wind, and barometric pressure, notes the ship’s latitude and longitude, and includes bearings to landmarks on the Californian coast, such as Cape Mendocino. The right page shows a hand-drawn coastline sketched from published reference maps and charts, on which the crew plotted the positions recorded in the log. In this way they traced the Swiftshure’s track down the coast from Esquimalt on the southern tip of Vancouver Island to San Francisco Bay.
