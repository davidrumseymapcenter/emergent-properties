Here the history of the American political system is depicted like a winding river. Parties of the first century of American history flow, merge, and split into tributaries, with all of the turbulence visible in one view. Color represents different political parties, width shows their strength, and the one holding power is positioned on top. The current smooths into one broad stream during the “Era of Good Feeling” where parties merged after 1817, and what follows twists into rapids during the decades leading up to the American Civil War. Houghton’s accomplishment in his chart, included in *Conspectus of The History of Political Parties and the Federal Government*, was to provide clarity at a glance about an important history impossible to memorize and hard to find summarized anywhere.
