Maps and data visualizations work together in *Lo sviluppo dell'industria italiana* (“The Development of Italian Industry”), a thematic national atlas of 109 charts and three maps tracing growth from 1876 to 1928. In the atlas, pictorial statistics are given a distinctly Italian flavor. On this plate, devoted to the food preserving industry, exports of tomato preserves grow into larger cans, while exports of salted and smoked meats, “lard excluded,” hang on lengthening racks of prosciutto and salami. The delight is real, and the messaging is memorable. Published under Mussolini’s regime by Italy’s industrial confederation, this atlas represents a nation that only grows: statistics working with the national self-portrait during Italy’s early Fascist period.
