Innovations of the Neuraths’ Isotype system are demonstrated here in the use of repeating units to show a numerical increase (as opposed to scaling the size of a pictogram, as in the adjacent Italian atlas) and elegant fusion of statistical accuracy with simplified representational elements. In *MOCKBA PEKOHCTPУИPУETCЯ — All-union Institute of Pictorial Statistics of Soviet Construction and Economy*, this Soviet planning atlas celebrates Stalin’s reconstruction of Moscow with visual simplicity and clarity. These pages show growth in the city’s number of buses and trolleys in the years leading up to 1938. The maps show the existing networks these modes of transportation operated, with the dotted line in the map on the right representing improvements projected for 1938. The quote below the map reads “The trolleybus keeps the city’s air clean; therefore, the trolleybus fleet in the new Moscow is increasing rapidly.” The full atlas’s 260 pages of persuasive civil-planning imagery and before-and-after evidence of the progress made in Stalin’s Moscow.
