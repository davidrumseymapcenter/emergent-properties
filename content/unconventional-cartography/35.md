*A Cultural Map: Southeast San Francisco* was conceived and designed by artists and is populated with places of importance to neighborhood residents, and its unconventional style of cartography measures a place by what its citizens care about most. Its contents were gathered during an exhibition at City College of San Francisco, labeled here just west of the Excelsior neighborhood, where visitors shared their ideas for what belonged on the map. Contributors’ names appear within the green outer border. The result is a subjective geography of the area: less of a record of the physical landscape than a portrait of community values, made by citizens, for citizens.
