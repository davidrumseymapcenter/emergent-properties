Hand-drawn on the back cover of a spiral-bound notepad, this map was not for commission or press, but to capture directions to the house of a friend. Artist and cartoonist Robert Crumb, perhaps best known for work on late 20th century underground comics series like Zap and characters like Mr. Natural, has traced his personal route from San Francisco, over the Golden Gate Bridge, to Muir Beach, noting the landmarks his memory actually keeps: Richfield station, a row of mailboxes, and a rope fence. Yet the mapmaker’s instincts are all there, like the compass rose, an old Spanish galleon sailing offshore, a vignette of Eric’s house above the water, all shown with a nod to any antique map. It’s not a polished piece, but a glimpse into the artist’s mind. Nonetheless, it does what all maps do: illustrate the world from a particular point of view.
