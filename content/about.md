## The David Rumsey Map Center

The [David Rumsey Map Center](https://library.stanford.edu/libraries/david-rumsey-map-center) is part of Stanford University Libraries. Its collections span the 15th century to the present and are not bounded by geography, ranging from early printed atlases and globes to manuscript surveys, data visualizations, and maps made by artists.

The Center is a public resource. Its collections are open to everyone, not only to the Stanford community. Through instruction, research support, and public programming, the Center invites its users to make their own discoveries and to think critically about what maps show and how they were made.

_Emergent Properties_ commemorates the tenth anniversary of the David Rumsey Map Center. It brings together forty objects that have, over the past decade, become drivers of scholarship, teaching, and programming at the Center: a selection of highlights that reflects what has mattered most to the people who use the collections.
