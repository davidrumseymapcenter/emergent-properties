This world map combines sophisticated geographic knowledge of some parts of the globe with distortions in others, including nonexistent continents at both North and South poles. It opens the first edition of what is considered the first modern atlas of the world, Abraham Ortelius’s *Theatrum Orbis Terrarum* (“Theater of the World”), which contained 53 maps specifically engraved for the volume with a consistent style, format, and lettering, and paired them with text descriptions of the world as Europeans knew it at the time. Its great commercial success set the standard for atlases and launched Ortelius as one of the most prolific of the celebrated Flemish cartographers. Unlike many surviving copies, this map was never hand-colored.
